01 — MATERIALS

What you'll need

  • Exterior-suitable lumber for the planter walls
  • Four wood corner supports
  • Exterior/deck screws appropriate for your lumber
  • Landscape fabric or planter liner, if desired
  • Exterior finish or stain, if desired
  • Raised-bed soil and plants

02 — CUT PLANNING

Build the cut list from your finished size.

Part Quantity Size
Long wall boards Based on height Finished planter length
End wall boards Based on height Finished width, adjusted for joint layout
Corner supports 4 Finished wall height
Optional top trim As needed Measure after assembly

Measure twice: dry-fit one layer before batch-cutting every piece.

03 — ASSEMBLY

Put it together.

  1. Choose and mark the finished footprint and height.
  2. Cut four identical corner supports.
  3. Cut and dry-fit the first wall layer.
  4. Clamp and fasten the long sides to the corner supports.
  5. Attach the ends and check the box for square.
  6. Add the remaining wall boards while keeping corners aligned.
  7. Add optional top trim after measuring the assembled box.
  8. Sand sharp edges and splinters.
  9. Apply your chosen exterior finish, let it cure, then fill and plant.
