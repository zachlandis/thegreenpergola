The Green Pergola story

Started in the backyard. Still very much in the backyard.

Green Pergola grew out of the simple idea that useful outdoor stuff shouldn’t have to look cheap, and good-looking outdoor stuff shouldn’t have to be precious.

So we build things the way we want them at home: sturdy, practical, easy to live with, and just polished enough to make the neighbors ask where you got it.
